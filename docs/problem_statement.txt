Backstory:
In the age of e-commerce, customer reviews are a critical factor in purchasing
decisions. However, popular products can accumulate hundreds or thousands of
reviews, making it impossible for a shopper to read them all. This information
overload leads to
'
analysis paralysis
'
or reliance on just a few top reviews, which
may not be representative of the overall consensus. Shoppers need a way to
quickly and accurately digest the collective opinion hidden within the text.
Problem Statement:
Develop
'Review Radar,
'
a web tool and browser extension that automates the
process of product review analysis. The platform
'
s objective is to ingest the URL
of an e-commerce product page, scrape its customer reviews, and apply a pretrained sentiment analysis model to generate a simple, actionable dashboard
that summarizes the overall public opinion and key product attributes.
Key Features:
URL-Based Web Scraper: A robust module that accepts a product page URL
from major e-commerce sites and automatically navigates and extracts the
text content of all customer reviews.
Pre-trained Sentiment Classifier: The core of the tool, which uses a pretrained sentiment analysis model (e.g., from Hugging Face) to classify each
scraped review as positive, negative, or neutral.
Keyword & Topic Extraction: An algorithm that performs topic modeling or
keyword extraction (like TF-IDF) on the positive and negative review sets to
identify the most frequently mentioned product features or issues (e.g.,
"battery,
" "
screen,
" "
customer service
").
Simple Summary Dashboard: A clean, easy-to-understand user interface that
visualizes the analysis with a sentiment breakdown pie chart (e.g.,
"85%
Positive
"), and a list of the top positive and negative keywords.
